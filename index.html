Apartment renovation questions

What New York landlords and owners ask us before a job starts.

Serving Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx and Staten Island.

How long does an apartment renovation take in NYC?

Our target for a typical unit is 45 days of construction, measured from the day the permit is issued and we have access. Permit and board approval time comes before that and varies by building and borough. Your written proposal states the schedule for your unit.

How much does an apartment renovation cost?

It depends on the unit size, the scope and the finish level, so we price every job after a site walk. You get one fixed price for the agreed scope in writing. If something outside that scope comes up, such as a hidden plumbing problem, we price it as a written change order before doing the work.

Which parts of New York City do you serve?

All five boroughs: Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx and Staten Island. We work in rental buildings, condos and co-ops.

Do you handle permits and board approvals?

Yes. We prepare and file the NYC Department of Buildings paperwork and put together the alteration package your co-op or condo board asks for. Approval timing is set by the city and the building, not by us.

Can you renovate a unit while the rest of the building is occupied?

Yes, that is most of our work. We follow the building's work hours, protect hallways and elevators, and keep the unit closed off so neighbors are disturbed as little as possible.

What does a unit renovation include?

Whatever the unit needs to rent or sell for more: kitchens, bathrooms, flooring, painting, lighting, electrical and plumbing updates, up to a full gut renovation. We also renovate several units at once for owners turning over a building.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. KAT Renovation LLC is a licensed and insured general contractor and an EPA Lead-Safe Certified Firm, which matters for buildings built before 1978. We send a certificate of insurance on request.
